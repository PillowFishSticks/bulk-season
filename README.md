# Bulk Season

A short, active incremental game about eating your way to a bigger bench. Raid a food market until your appetite runs out, then burn the calories on the gym's skill tree and go back hungrier.

Play: https://pillowfishsticks.github.io/bulk-season/

## The loop

1. **Run.** Walk into food to eat it before your *Appetite* runs out. Chain bites quickly to build a combo.
2. **Stomach.** Every bite fills you up and digestion slowly empties you. You can always keep eating, but the fuller you are, the slower you chew and walk.
3. **Sections and bosses.** Each market has three sections (Corner Shop: Front Door, The Aisles, The Till), each with a boss that fights back with shots, slams and charges. Hits cost you appetite time. Beat a boss to open the next section; beat the third to open the next market.
4. **Gym.** Spend kcal on the skill tree. Run your own gym too: equipment brings members, members pay you money even while you're away, and money buys more equipment and supplements that boost you.
5. **New season.** Clear The Buffet and you can start again with medals that boost every kcal for good.

Each market has its own hazards. None of them can kill you, but they knock you about: wet floors, seagulls that nick your chips, chilli spills that flare up, scooters, waiters and Big Dave, who's eating too.

## Running it

It's one file with no build step. Open `index.html` in any browser, or host the repo with GitHub Pages.

Progress saves by itself in your browser after every run and purchase. To play on another device, open **Save & devices** in the gym, copy the code, and load it on the other device.

Using an Xbox controller? Press any button once so the browser picks it up. Plate Toss aims with the right stick, and LB/RB switch markets in the gym.
