How to play

🍔Eat

Knock down food monsters, then walk over the food they drop to eat it before your appetite runs out. Keep it going for a combo.

🎳Throw

You throw dumbbells on your own: the boss first, then the nearest food monster. Grab power-ups for rapid fire, spread shots and more.

👑Beat the boss

Every market has 5 sections. Eat your way past the food goblins until the boss turns up, then beat it before your appetite runs out. Getting hit costs you time.

🏋️Get stronger

Spend kcal on the Skill Tree. Run Your Gym too: equipment brings members, members pay £, and £ buys supplements.
