How to play

🍔Eat

Walk into food before your appetite runs out. Your stomach fills as you eat and empties as you digest. Keep chomping for a combo.

👑Beat the boss

Eat enough and the market's boss turns up. It keeps its damage between runs. Beat it to open the next market.

🏋️Get stronger

Spend kcal on the Skill Tree. That's where Dash, Big Inhale, Plate Toss and Cheat Meal unlock.
