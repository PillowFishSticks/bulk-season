How to play

🍔Eat

Walk into food before your appetite runs out. Keep chomping without a break to build a combo for bonus kcal.

👑Beat the boss

Every market has 3 sections, each with a boss that fights back. Dodge its attacks. Beat it to open the next section.

🏋️Get stronger

Spend kcal on the Skill Tree. Run Your Gym too: equipment brings members, members pay £, and £ buys supplements.
