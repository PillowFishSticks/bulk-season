How to play

Eat as much as you can before your Appetite runs out. Every bite fills your Stomach and digestion slowly empties it. When you're stuffed you can't eat. Keep chomping without a break to build a combo for bonus kcal.

Eat enough in a market and its boss turns up. Bosses keep their damage between runs. Beat one to earn a trophy (+10% kcal) and open the next market.

Back at the gym, spend kcal on the Skill Tree. That's where Dash, Big Inhale, Plate Toss and Cheat Meal unlock.
