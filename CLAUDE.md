# Bulk Season: notes for Claude

The owner (PillowFishSticks) isn't a developer and wants Claude to own the code. Keep it simple and cheap to maintain: no frameworks and no runtime dependencies. The web version has no build step. Electron is only used for the desktop wrapper (see Desktop).

## What it is
An active incremental web game modelled on **Chef Knight** (Steam). The loop is: short timed run → collect a resource → spend it at a hub on a skill tree → go deeper. It's themed on eating food and the gym. It is **desktop-first** (landscape, fullscreen, keyboard or Xbox controller), and it still works on a phone with touch drag.

## Look and feel (match Chef Knight)
- Hand-drawn cartoon style: thick dark outlines (`OUT` #2a1a12), a dark brick and stone dungeon, warm lamp glow and a vignette.
- UI is wooden plaques and parchment panels. Fonts are Alegreya (display) and Alegreya Sans (UI).
- Foods are emoji turned into outlined "stickers" by `sprite()`, with googly eyes that track the player. Runners get little legs. Hits flash white and squash.
- The lifter is drawn procedurally in `drawLifter()`: chibi build, walk cycle, a belly that grows as you eat through a run, a chomping mouth and dizzy eyes.

## Files
- `index.html` is the whole game: CSS, markup and JS in one file.
- `README.md` is the player-facing description.
- `fonts/` holds the self-hosted Alegreya fonts (OFL), so the game works offline. The page no longer uses Google Fonts.
- `desktop/main.js` + `desktop/preload.js` + `desktop/icon.png` form the Electron wrapper. `package.json` holds electron-builder's config.

## Code map (inside `<script>`)
- `ZONES`: five markets. Each has a `cost`, `at` (foods eaten in a run before its boss appears), a theme `th`, `foods`, a `boss` and hazards `hz`. It also has `tasks`, which are done in order and pay a flat kcal reward.
- `F(emoji, name, fill, kcal, {w, bites, flee, spicy, size})` defines a food. `fill` and `kcal` cover the whole item.
- Sections and bosses: each market has 5 `secs` (`NSEC`) (own floor and wall drawing, food ×`SECK`, more hazards), and each section has one boss. Beat a section's boss to unlock the next section (`S.sec[zone]`, `S.cleared["zone:i"]`); beating the fifth clears the market (`S.beaten`, a trophy) and unlocks the next market for free. Markets have no kcal cost: kcal only buys upgrades, and boss HP is the gate. Boss HP comes from the market's `bb.hps` (one number per section, auto-tuned with the sim on 2026-10-17 so the bot needs 2–3 runs for **every** section; the owner found sections 2–5 only took one run). Each boss keeps a fixed kcal pool `kt` (the old totals, via `hp0`/`BHP0`) and pays `kc = kt/hp` per point of damage, so a tougher boss never speeds up the economy. Rewards use `BREW`. To retune: change `hps`, run `BS.sim`, and look at runs per section (the sim log's `fight`, `hp` and `dps` fields help). Bosses reset to full HP every run (the owner found persistent HP weird). Size them so a player who arrives with fair upgrades can beat one in about 15–20s of chewing; S.bhp is unused. A beaten boss stays beaten: in a cleared section, filling the bar gives a golden feast (`feast()`), not another boss. 
- Boss attacks (`atk` on each boss, run by `bossAttacks()`): spread, ring, spin (spiral), slam (telegraphed circle) and charge (telegraphed line). Hits cost appetite time (shot 1.5s, slam/charge 2.5s) with a red screen flash. Shots are slow and glow red (radius 15; every attack with 3+ shots fires 30% fewer than its `n`, so late fights stay dodgeable); a near miss pays a "Dodge!" kcal bonus. Dash makes you immune.
- There is no stomach any more (the owner disliked it). The red branch is Iron Stomach (×kcal), Second Helpings (+time per food, max +15s/run) and Padding. Cheat Meal makes food worth ×3. The HUD's second bar is the combo timer.
- Events branch (purple nodes): Plate Drop (`drop`, plates fall on food clusters every few seconds and smash them), Gym Buddy (`buddy`, friendly lifters in `R.buds` eat food for you) and Gains Train (`chain`, a chance for a chomp to zap up to 3 nearby foods). Run by `events()`, drawn by `drawEvents()`.
- Foods get chewier per market (`CHEWY`), and on top of that **`EHP[market][section]`** multiplies every food monster's HP (goblins ×1.5 more; `f.hp0` is the spawn HP, used by the HP bar). It was set from the sim's `ov` field (median dumbbell damage ÷ spawn HP) so a hit does ~40% of a regular enemy, 2–3 hits each, everywhere (owner: everything was one-shot, up to 150× overkill late). Kcal comes only from loot, so tougher enemies don't change the payout. If you touch damage, re-measure `ov` (target ~0.4) and rescale `EHP`. The sim log's `hpk` is all hits per kill (buddies included).
- Your Gym (hub tab, `TAB`): `EQUIP` levels (`S.g`) give members (`members()`, boosted by `fame()` = trophies + bench), and members pay £0.25/s each (`income()`) into `S.cash`. That money buys `EQUIP` and `SUPPS` (`S.sp`). **Supplements are gym-only perks, nothing the tree gives** (owner request): Training partner (`R.mate`, a second lifter who throws darts with `m` power), Spotter (`R.spot` free hits, checked first in `knock()`), One More Set (`R.second`, extra seconds at time-up, can pass 25s), Hype man (`R.hype`, boss needs 5%/level fewer kills), Magnet straps (`R.mag`, power-up pickup reach + pull), Rest timer (`R.cdK` skill cooldowns) and Prize money (£ per boss kill, `R.prize`). The old six stat supplements were removed; `migrate()` (`v6`) refunds their £. The Gym bag (start a run with a power-up) was removed too (owner found it dumb); `v7` refunds it. **Nothing in the gym is locked behind a market** (owner, 2026-10-17: being stuck on a market froze the gym, and unlocking a market meant a burst of clicking with saved-up money). Prices alone pace it: the bot buys something after ~80% of runs and is still buying at The Buffet. The Gear Guy (id still `pool`, 12 levels, ×2.2; owner's idea, replacing a swimming pool that drew as a stray blue strip) is the late sink and carries into later seasons. He's drawn in `drawGym()` as a shady lifter in a dark hoodie and cap by GEAR crates (more crates as he levels) between the bikes and the smoothie bar. Keep it a wink: never spell out steroids (Steam age ratings). `gymNeed`/`gymLocked`/`gymIsNew`/`seeNew` are stubs that return 0/false (the NEW badge and "New in Your Gym" message are gone with the locks); the `t` tiers on `EQUIP` are unused. **Gym ranks** (`RANKS`, `rankN()`) unlock by member count, placed on the sim's member curve: Bronze 40 (~Corner Shop, +15% kcal), Silver 300 (~Chippy, supplements 20% cheaper, in `gcost`), Gold 1500 (~Curry House, +25% throw damage), Platinum 5000 (~Street Food, power-ups last 50% longer), Legend 15000 (~Buffet, ×1.5 kcal). The sim log's `gym m… r… eq… sp…` fields show this per run. Shown as `#ranks` (`renderRanks`); a banner fires on rank-up.
- Nudges (owner wants players to notice the gym, like phone games): `badges()` puts red counters on the Skill Tree and Your Gym tabs (`data-n`, how many things you can afford right now; they bob while unselected). The results card shows `#rGym` (what the gym earned during the run + how many gym upgrades are ready) and a `#gymBtn2` Your Gym button that turns gold when something's affordable. Money accrues all the time, at full rate while the tab is hidden and at half rate offline (up to 8h, `catchUp`). `drawGym()` draws the gym scene with equipment slots and wandering members. A new season keeps the gym and cash but resets supplements.
- Hazards ramp up per section: `R.stage` (0–2) adds seagulls, fires, puddles and waiters, and `R.hzF` speeds up spawns.
- `NODES` is the skill tree on a grid (x = column, y = row, start at 0,0). Every parent-child link is exactly one step up, down, left or right, so lines never cross and the d-pad is predictable; keep that rule when adding nodes. It renders in a pan/zoom viewport (`TV`, `zoomBy`, `fitTree`, `homeTree`, `followNode`: wheel, +/-, right stick, drag, two-finger pinch via `PT`/`pinch`). **Don't auto-recentre it** (the owner hated it snapping back): only the very first size centres on the root; after that the ResizeObserver keeps the same point in the middle, and switching tabs leaves the view alone. The `#info` panel under the tree has a fixed height (description clamped to 2 lines, the Buy button hidden with `visibility`), so hovering nodes never moves the layout. A late-game sink, Bulk Up (`bulk`, 4,1 under Crushing Bites, 40 levels, ×1.4 cost), keeps kcal useful once the rest of the tree is bought. Nodes you can't open yet show as a "?" when their parent is reachable (`peek`) and are hidden beyond that. Directions: up = TIME (appetite, time drops/clocks, less time lost), right = DAMAGE (chew, jaw, power, crit, combo), left = GAINS (kcal multipliers, golden food, crowds, feasts), down = ABILITIES (footwork/reach, the 4 skills and their upgrades, purple events). Nodes have 3–5 levels, and a child opens when its parent reaches `need()` = min(3, parent max). Prices are set per market (shop ~40–600, chippy ~5k–25k, curry ~200k–1M, street ~20–60M, buffet ~1B+), so the bot ends a season with most of the tree. Fields: `p`, `x`/`y` (100×64 grid), `max`, `base`, `k`, `v(level)`, `f(value)`.
- Runs are capped at 30 seconds of real play (`MAXRUN`, owner request: the cap, not the usual length). Start time = 15s + Appetite/Bottomless/Pre-workout, capped at 30. **Build-up then boss** (owner, 2026-10-17: the boss used to arrive 1–2s in): the boss comes after `secAt()` kills (`zone.at`: 10/23/42/52/56, +8% per section; counted when loot is eaten, `R.prog`). The run opens with at most 10 enemies (`spawnFood(true)`), and the rest arrive at the spawn rate, so the build-up takes ~7s at the shop to ~10s at the buffet for the bot (sim log `bAt el/T kN`). A backup timer spawns it at 60% of the run. Time gains (clocks, Second Helpings, Second Wind) go through `addTime()` and can only win back time lost to hits (`R.el` tracks elapsed time). If you haven't lost any time, a clock gives bonus kcal instead. Iron Will (top of the Time branch) is a chance to shrug off a hit. 
- Bosses can't be AFK'd: standing next to one for over 2.2s triggers a telegraphed "bump" shockwave (-2s), and below 50% HP they enrage (faster attacks and moves).
- Beating a section boss ends the run as a win (`R.win`): the character jumps with arms up (`drawCheer`) under "BOSS DOWN!" with confetti, and the feast is paid as bonus kcal. Characters (`CHARS`, `S.char`, picker in the Character modal) are free and purely cosmetic: options passed to `drawLifter` (sk, top:"bare", crop, pony, ears, muzzle, stem, visor, antenna, cap, whistle, bm). Boss attack kinds: spread, ring, spin, slam, charge, wave (gap), rain, homing, burst, boomer, bounce, mine. Each boss has its own mix. Goblins shoot per market (`GOB`/`goblinShot`).
- End of run: `R.ending` freezes play for 1.6s, zooms in, and the lifter kneels panting (`drawKneel`) under "OUT OF STEAM!". Then the results card slides up. Beating The Sunday Roast leads to the closing credits (`showCredits`, a CSS crawl) and the option to start the next season.
- Crits: every chomp has 5% (+Big Bite) to deal ×3. Belly Slam (`bslam`, base 4% per chomp) sends a shockwave ring that bites every food nearby and hits bosses (`bellySlam()`, drawn from `R.pounds`).
- Food goblins (`zone.mini`, flag `f.mini`) wander and shoot single shots on the way to the boss, and are edible for bonus kcal. Time drops (`CLOCK`, flag `f.clock`) add appetite when eaten.
- Each market's room is drawn in `buildRoom()` from `FLOORS[id]` and `WALLS[id]` (shop shelves and fridges, chippy tiles, menus and hot counter, curry arches and carpet, street night market, buffet table and chandeliers). Light fittings are drawn every frame in `drawLights()`. kcal scales with the market, so earlier markets pay less.
- Hazards are updated in `hazards()`: puddles/grease (slide or slow), fire spills, seagulls (steal food; run into them to shoo), scooters (warning lane, then a knock), waiters (bump them to drop food) and Big Dave (a rival eater). A knock means stun plus lost time, never death.
- The save is `localStorage` key `bulkSeason.v2` (`fresh()` lists the fields). Bump the key if the save shape changes incompatibly. "Save & devices" exports the whole save as a `BULK1:` + base64 code (copy or download) and imports one. This is how the owner moves between laptop and phone; there are no accounts.
- Save safety: every load copies the raw save to `bulkSeason.v2.backup` (Save & devices → Restore backup). If loading throws, `S.loadFailed` stops saving so the real save is never overwritten. A `storage` event from another tab sets `readOnly`, so an older tab can't clobber newer progress. When testing, block the old page's `setItem` before reloading, or its unload save overwrites the test save.
- Audio: `audio()` makes the context on the first click or key press. `SFX` respects `S.muted`, and the music respects `S.music` independently. Music is electro dance (owner request): a step sequencer (`SONGS.hub` 118 bpm / `SONGS.run` 128 bpm, Am–F–C–G, 64 sixteenth-note steps) scheduled by `musicTick`, with `setSong()` switching tracks per screen. `mStep` plays a four-on-the-floor kick, claps on 2 and 4, off-beat hats, a pad that ducks on every kick (the "pump"), off-beat saw bass, a 16th arp in runs and a detuned-saw lead (`mSaw`, `mNoise`).
- A "How to play" modal opens on the first visit (`S.runs===0`).
- **Food monsters and loot** (owner request, 2026-10-17, second pass). Every food in `R.foods` (except bosses and clocks) is now an enemy: it shuffles toward you (`f.goal`/`f.spd`); touching it only pushes you apart. Within 150px it winds up a **lunge** (0.5s red circle and direction line drawn in `drawFood`, then a 0.22s dash that costs 0.5s on a hit, cooldown 2.2–3.4s: `f.lunge`/`f.ldash`/`f.lcd`), so hits are readable instead of random; and is never eaten directly. Knocking one down (`eaten()`) drops **loot** (`spawnLoot` → `R.loot`). Loot never sits on the floor (the owner found it looked like more enemies): after a 0.18s pop it homes into you with a trail and is eaten on arrival (`lootTick` → `eatLoot`), which is where kcal, combo, tasks (`S.eat`), golden effects, Second Helpings, spicy boosts, Belly Slam chances and the boss counter (`R.prog`) now live. `KLOOT` scales loot kcal. Enemy HP is still `def.bites` (×`CHEWY` per market), so Grip Strength (old Jaw) matters. Damage per hit is `hitAmt(f)`: bosses `jaw×dmg×thrPow` (with `THR`), food monsters `jaw×thrPowE` (no `THR`). Enemies are on screen from the start.
- Skills reworked for this: Dash smashes monsters for 2× damage; **Shield** (skill id still `inhale`, so saves keep it; replaced Big Inhale, which the owner found underwhelming): for 3s (+0.5s per Mirror Shield level, `inhaleR` = radius 150 +15%/level) you can't be hit, enemy shots inside it break, and every 0.25s `shield()` hits every food monster inside for 1.5× (bosses 0.5×) and shoves them out; drawn as a blue bubble with spinning plates. Its upgrade **Mirror Shield** (`inhaleR`, owner's idea, kept as an upgrade so the base Shield stays simple) reflects enemy shots back at their shooter (`p.src`, else the boss, else the nearest enemy) as blue-glow dumbbells: at most `reflMax()` = 2+2×level per shield, each at `REFL_DMG` 60% (balance: a boss spiral fires ~35 shots); **Gym Party** (skill id still `toss`, E/X; owner's idea, replaced Plate Toss, which felt pointless): 3 mates (+1 per Bigger Party `tossN` level) run in from the sides, circle you (`R.party`, `PARTY_LOOKS`) and each throws a dumbbell every 0.4s at `thrTarget()` for 4s (+0.5s/level), then run off; each dumbbell is `PARTY_M` (0.5) × one of yours incl. `R.thrK`. 10s cooldown. They kneel/cheer at the end of a run like your partner; Cheat Meal makes food ×3 and throws 80% faster. Plate Drop hits crowds for 5×. Gains Train chains from dumbbell hits. Gym Buddies (tree node `buddy`, renamed to say it's bought in the tree) punch food monsters (0.8× a hit every 0.45s). Big Dave steals kills (he eats monsters, no loot for you). Long arms now extends power-up/clock pickup. Seagulls still target loot when there is any, otherwise they dive at you. Waiters drop loot; feasts rain golden loot.
- Seagulls are enemies: they swoop in to steal loot (`st:"in"`) or dive at you (`st:"dive"`, a peck costs 0.6s). `thrTarget()` includes them, and dumbbells hit them via `hitGull()`; a downed gull drops what it carried.
- Bosses enter from the top, left or right to a random spot away from you (`f.tx/f.ty`, lerped in `bossMove` while `f.enter>0`).
- Tree colours: each node wears its branch colour (`BRC`, `--c`): Time blue, Damage orange, Gains red, Skills purple, Events pink (the `g` keys are still "green"/"purple"; only `BRC` maps them). **Never use green for a branch**: the owner read green as "buyable". Lines to open nodes take the child's branch colour, with a key (`#tlegend`). Affordable nodes get a white pulse and a red "!" badge; maxed get a gold halo. Damage nodes were renamed for throwing (Quick Hands, Grip Strength, Fast Hands, Iron Grip, Power/Crushing Throws, Big Hit).
- **Eat food, throw at enemies** (owner request, 2026-10-17). Walking into food eats it, but bosses and goblins are never eaten: the eat loop and dash skip `f.boss||f.mini`. Instead `throwing()` auto-fires dumbbells (`R.darts`, drawn by `drawDumbbell`) every `R.thrCd` (= chew × Fast Jaws / Quick Release, so chewing upgrades speed up throws too). `thrTarget()` picks the boss if it's within `thrR+260`, otherwise the nearest goblin within `thrR`, and fires nothing when no enemy is in range. The aimed dumbbell homes gently on its target; Twin Throw extras fan out without homing. A hit calls `chomp(f,{shot:1,amt,crit})`: damage = jaw × (boss: `R.dmg`) × `R.thrPow`, where thrPow = `THR` × `THRZ[market]` × Throw Power × Heavy Iron. `THR`/`THRZ` exist because throws never miss and never need you close; they were tuned with the sim so boss fights last about as long as chewing did (shop ~4s, chippy 6–10s, curry/street 8–15s, buffet 7–10s for the bot).
- **Throw cap and readability** (owner: late game you couldn't see yourself): at most 4 throws a second (`THR_GAP` .25s); when throw-speed stats and Rapid Fire/Cheat Meal would go faster, the extra becomes damage (`R.thrK` = gap/eff, multiplied into each dart's `m`; partner capped at 0.4s with the same trick, `noK` on its darts). Dumbbells come from alternating hands (`R.hand`), not the mouth. Draw order: enemy shots above dumbbells, dumbbells within 90px of you at 55% alpha, a floor ring at your feet, then `drawPlayer` last so you're always on top. Boss kcal floats are summed and shown every 0.45s (`R.bossKc`).
- Throwing nodes (yellow, grid-adjacent): tpow (1,1 under chew), quick (2,2 under combo) → trange (2,3) → tspd (2,4); quick → twin (3,2) → pierce (4,2) → heavy (5,2); trange → pdrop (3,3) → pump (3,4).
- Power-ups (`PUPS`, `R.pups`, `R.pw`, `powerups()`): rapid (×2.5 fire rate), spread (+4 dumbbells), ring (8 extra in a circle), heavy (×2.5 damage, bigger, and each dumbbell ricochets to the nearest un-hit enemy up to 3 times: `d.bounce`). One spawns every `11-1.5×Power-up Drops` seconds and goblins drop one 12% (+6%/level) of the time. They last `Pumped` seconds (4 + level). Active ones show as timer rings above the player (`drawPwIcons`).
- Goblins now come in packs (3–7 depending on section and market), drift toward the player, bump you for 0.8s on contact, and each shoots a bit less often than before. Gym Buddies leave goblins alone.
- `startRun` / `update` / `chomp` / `eaten` / `draw` / `endRun` make up the run. A single `frame()` loop also polls the gamepad and animates the hub.
- Screens: a "Press any key/button" title (`#title`, `leaveTitle()`; a key or click also requests fullscreen), then the gym hub, the run, and modals. On landscape screens, `fitScreen()` adds `body.staged`: the hub is laid out at a fixed 1600×900 `#stage` and scaled with `--k` to fit (no scrolling, like a console game). The run HUD scales with `--hk`, and modal cards with `--mk`, so it all reads well on a TV. Landscape phones (`body.phone`, when the 1600×900 stage would scale below 0.62) get a smaller stage, `--sw`×`--sh`, that fills the screen at a readable scale. On phones nothing scrolls: `fitCard()` (a ResizeObserver on every `.mcard`) scales each menu card to fit the screen, and Help and Save get wide phone layouts. On phones the hub becomes one full-width panel. `phoneDom()` moves `#goBtn` into the top bar and moves the market card and `.notes` (tasks) into a phone-only Markets tab (`#paneMk`, `TAB==="markets"`). The tool buttons and footer collapse into a ☰ Menu modal (`#menuModal`), the stats card and gym canvas are hidden, and on touch, tapping a tree node first selects it (`touchSel`) and a second tap buys it. Portrait on a touch screen shows `#rotate` ("turn your phone sideways"), which is pure CSS via `orientation:portrait and pointer:coarse`, and rotating mid-run pauses. Portrait on a desktop browser still falls back to the scrolling layout. Scrollbars, text selection and the context menu are disabled. The cursor hides when a pad is in use or the mouse is idle during a run.
- The hub's tree and market list are built once. `renderGym()` only updates classes and text, so controller focus survives purchases.
- Alerts (`banner()`, `#banner`) are a compact card in a corner, never the middle of the screen (owner: centre alerts blocked play): top-left under the HUD during runs (scaled by `--hk`), top-right in the hub (class `hub`, scaled by `--k`). The results card's Upgrade and Your Gym buttons carry the same red counters as the hub tabs (`data-n` from `treeCan()`/`gymCan()`).
- Controller/keyboard selection: a thick white ring with a dark edge plus rippling waves (`@keyframes selWave` for buttons, tabs, gym items, sections and characters; `@keyframes sel` for round tree nodes). Each wave has a white and a dark band so it shows on both parchment and dark panels.
- When a run ends, your Training partner and Gym Buddies kneel and pant with you (or cheer on a boss win): `drawKneel`/`drawCheer` take `{look,face,ph}` (`MATE_LOOK`, `BUD_LOOK`) and only the player gets the title.
- **First-time flow** (`S.tut`: 0 = play your first run, 1 = open your gym, 2 = normal; old saves with runs migrate to 2). A red coach bubble (`#coach`, `coach()`, fixed-position over the target, refreshed on the 0.25s hub tick) says "Play your first run!" over the Go button; Your Gym tab is greyed and blocked in `setTab`. The first results card hides Run it back/Upgrade and shows only "🏋️ Open your gym" (`openGym()`: free Free weights L1, banner, Gym tab). Before that there's no income (`income()` returns 0 while `S.tut<2`, except in the sim) and the £ plaque is hidden, then the bubble points at the Skill Tree (`S.coachTree`) until it's clicked.
- (Old, now off) **Gym unlock news:** clearing a market sets `R.newMkt`; the results card's `#rGym` (class `unlock`) lists that market's new equipment plus "the next level of every supplement". Newly opened items wear a gold NEW corner badge (`gymIsNew`: unlocked by a market after `S.gymSeen`, and not yet in `S.seenNew`); hovering, selecting or buying an item marks it seen (`seeNew`, key `id:m<market>`: per unlock, not per level, so buying a level never makes the next level look new). The Your Gym tab shows a gold NEW tag (`data-new`) while any are unseen. The bot buys these as they unlock, and boss HP is tuned assuming you do.
- Reset progress needs two presses and then a confirm modal (`#resetModal`, Cancel focused by default).
- Prestige: once The Buffet is beaten, "New season" resets the season and grants medals (+15% kcal each, `medalsFor`). Boss HP scales ×1.6 per season.

## Controls
- Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, Space/Shift Dash, Q Inhale, E Gym Party, R Cheat Meal, Esc/P pause, F fullscreen. Arrows and Enter navigate menus.
- Xbox pad: left stick or d-pad to move, A/RB Dash, B/LB Inhale, X Toss (aim with the right stick), Y Cheat, Start to pause. Rumble (`rumble()`) fires on knocks, golden food and bosses. In menus, d-pad/stick do spatial navigation (`navMove`), A presses, B backs out, LB/RB switch market, LT/RT (Q/E on keyboard) jump between the markets and the tree/gym lists (`jumpPanel`), and Start begins a run. In the tree the d-pad moves in straight lines along the grid (`treeMove`: same row/column first, then a narrow cone; it no longer follows parent links round corners, which made the owner buy the wrong node). Holding A keeps buying, but only on the node A was pressed on, after 0.6s, and never while a direction is held (`padHoldEl`). A toast confirms when a controller connects. Browsers only expose a pad after one button press, and a pad press doesn't count as a user gesture for fullscreen, so use F or a click for that.
- Touch (`body.touch`, set on the first touch or on `pointer:coarse` devices): an on-screen controller. The first finger on the arena becomes a floating stick (`VS`, `#vstick`, follows the thumb past the rim), and the skills form an Xbox-style face-button cluster at bottom right (Dash = A, the biggest). Pause sits at bottom centre and reacts on pointerdown. The arena uses the full height under the HUD, and the controls overlay it. Tapping the title goes fullscreen (on `click`, since Chrome only grants fullscreen when a tap ends, not on touch pointerdown) and locks landscape where allowed (Android). A ⛶ Fullscreen button sits on the title (`#tfsBtn`) and beside pause during a run while not fullscreen (`#runFs`); `body.fs` / `body.nofs` (iPhone has no fullscreen API) control them. Touch controls scale with `--ck` on short screens. `manifest.webmanifest` (fullscreen, landscape) makes "Add to Home screen" launch like an app.
- Glyph hints switch with the last input device (`body.pad` / `body.touch`).

## Balance
Open `index.html#debug` and run `BS.sim(900)` in the console. A bot plays full seasons headlessly and buys the cheapest upgrade each time, then returns milestones (run number and minutes at each market open and boss kill). Tuning targets (owner, 2026-10-17): **every section should take 2, maybe 3 runs (aim 2.5)**. The bot averages ~2.5–3 per section (any single section varies 1–5 with luck), so season 1 is about 58–62 runs (~33 bot minutes, re-tuned 2026-10-17 after the build-up/30s change) and earns ~5 medals (`medalsFor` divides by 4.5e9). Season 2 with 6 medals is about the same length (boss HP ×1.5 per season) and earns ~12. The sim log now shows `fight Ns` (how long each boss took): use it when touching throw damage. The sim also earns and spends gym money. `BS.sim(900,{season:2,medals:5})` checks later seasons: about 20 bot minutes each, earning 5–6 medals.

## Desktop (Electron)
- In the page, `window.desktop` (from preload) means the game is running on desktop. There, saves go through `desktop.load()`/`desktop.save()` to `%APPDATA%/Bulk Season/save.json` (an atomic tmp+rename write, with `save.backup.json` copied on every launch) instead of localStorage. A Quit button is shown (`#quitGame`, `#quitGame2`). A single-instance lock stops two copies from fighting over the save.
- F3 toggles an FPS counter.
- Benchmarks: `npm run fps` (uncapped, about 200 FPS) or `npx electron . --fps-vsync` (should hold 143–144 on a 144 Hz screen with about 2 dropped frames). They print `FPS TEST {...}` and use a temporary profile.
- Build: `npm run dist` → `dist/*-portable.exe` and `*-setup.exe`. They're unsigned. Set `CSC_IDENTITY_AUTO_DISCOVERY=false`.
- **In Claude's shell, `ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE=1` is set (VS Code)**, so always prefix electron/electron-builder commands with `env -u ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE`.
- Performance rule: the run HUD must only touch the DOM when a value changes. Use `hset(sel,prop,val)`, which is cached in `HC` and cleared in `startRun`. Bars use a `transform: scaleX` (`bar` prop), never `width`. Per-frame DOM writes are what cost the frame rate (117 → 144 FPS).
- Steam later: the plan is steamworks.js for achievements and Steam Cloud (point Cloud at save.json), a separate free Demo app, and real art in place of the emoji icon.

## Testing

Load the page at 1920×1080 (the owner plays fullscreen on a TV), 1366×768 and 390×844, press a key past the title, start a run, sweep with the arrow keys, wait for the results screen, and check for page errors and no horizontal overflow on `#gym`.
