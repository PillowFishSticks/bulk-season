# Bulk Season: notes for Claude

The owner (PillowFishSticks) isn't a developer and wants Claude to own the code. Keep it simple and cheap to maintain: no frameworks and no runtime dependencies. The web version has no build step. Electron is only used for the desktop wrapper (see Desktop).

## What it is
An active incremental web game modelled on **Chef Knight** (Steam). The loop is: short timed run → collect a resource → spend it at a hub on a skill tree → go deeper. It's themed on eating food and the gym. It is **desktop-first** (landscape, fullscreen, keyboard or Xbox controller), and it still works on a phone with touch drag.

## Look and feel (match Chef Knight)
- Hand-drawn cartoon style: thick dark outlines (`OUT` #2a1a12), a dark brick and stone dungeon, warm lamp glow and a vignette.
- UI is wooden plaques and parchment panels. Fonts are Alegreya (display) and Alegreya Sans (UI).
- Foods are emoji turned into outlined "stickers" by `sprite()`, with googly eyes that track the player. Runners get little legs. Hits flash white and squash.
- The lifter is drawn procedurally in `drawLifter()`: chibi build, walk cycle, a belly that grows as you eat through a run, a chomping mouth and dizzy eyes.

## Files
- `index.html` is the whole game: CSS, markup and JS in one file.
- `README.md` is the player-facing description.
- `fonts/` holds the self-hosted Alegreya fonts (OFL), so the game works offline. The page no longer uses Google Fonts.
- `desktop/main.js` + `desktop/preload.js` + `desktop/icon.png` form the Electron wrapper. `package.json` holds electron-builder's config.

## Code map (inside `<script>`)
- `ZONES`: five markets. Each has a `cost`, `at` (foods eaten in a run before its boss appears), a theme `th`, `foods`, a `boss` and hazards `hz`. It also has `tasks`, which are done in order and pay a flat kcal reward.
- `F(emoji, name, fill, kcal, {w, bites, flee, spicy, size})` defines a food. `fill` and `kcal` cover the whole item.
- Sections and bosses: each market has 5 `secs` (`NSEC`) (own floor and wall drawing, food ×`SECK`, more hazards), and each section has one boss. Beat a section's boss to unlock the next section (`S.sec[zone]`, `S.cleared["zone:i"]`); beating the fifth clears the market (`S.beaten`, a trophy) and unlocks the next market for free. Markets have no kcal cost: kcal only buys upgrades, and boss HP is the gate. Boss HP/reward come from the market's `bb` (×`BHP`/`BREW` per section). Bosses reset to full HP every run (the owner found persistent HP weird). Size them so a player who arrives with fair upgrades can beat one in about 15–20s of chewing; S.bhp is unused. A beaten boss stays beaten: in a cleared section, filling the bar gives a golden feast (`feast()`), not another boss. 
- Boss attacks (`atk` on each boss, run by `bossAttacks()`): spread, ring, spin (spiral), slam (telegraphed circle) and charge (telegraphed line). Hits cost appetite time (shot 1.5s, slam/charge 2.5s) with a red screen flash. Shots are big, slow and glow red; a near miss pays a "Dodge!" kcal bonus. Dash makes you immune.
- There is no stomach any more (the owner disliked it). The red branch is Iron Stomach (×kcal), Second Helpings (+time per food, max +15s/run) and Padding. Cheat Meal makes food worth ×3. The HUD's second bar is the combo timer.
- Events branch (purple nodes): Plate Drop (`drop`, plates fall on food clusters every few seconds and smash them), Gym Buddy (`buddy`, friendly lifters in `R.buds` eat food for you) and Gains Train (`chain`, a chance for a chomp to zap up to 3 nearby foods). Run by `events()`, drawn by `drawEvents()`.
- Foods get chewier per market (`CHEWY`: more bites with the same kcal and fill per bite), so Jaw strength keeps mattering.
- Your Gym (hub tab, `TAB`): `EQUIP` levels (`S.g`) give members (`members()`, boosted by `fame()` = trophies + bench), and members pay £ per second (`income()`) into `S.cash`. That money buys `EQUIP` and `SUPPS` (`S.sp`: shake +kcal, pre-workout +time, creatine +boss damage, trainer +speed, electrolytes -time lost to hits, socks +gold). Money accrues all the time, at full rate while the tab is hidden and at half rate offline (up to 8h, `catchUp`). `drawGym()` draws the gym scene with equipment slots and wandering members. A new season keeps the gym and cash but resets supplements.
- Hazards ramp up per section: `R.stage` (0–2) adds seagulls, fires, puddles and waiters, and `R.hzF` speeds up spawns.
- `NODES` is the skill tree on a grid (x = column, y = row, start at 0,0). Every parent-child link is exactly one step up, down, left or right, so lines never cross and the d-pad is predictable; keep that rule when adding nodes. It renders in a pan/zoom viewport (`TV`, `zoomBy`, `fitTree`, `homeTree`, `followNode`: wheel, +/-, right stick, drag, two-finger pinch via `PT`/`pinch`). A ResizeObserver recentres it when the tree area changes size. Nodes you can't open yet show as a "?" when their parent is reachable (`peek`) and are hidden beyond that. Directions: up = TIME (appetite, time drops/clocks, less time lost), right = DAMAGE (chew, jaw, power, crit, combo), left = GAINS (kcal multipliers, golden food, crowds, feasts), down = ABILITIES (footwork/reach, the 4 skills and their upgrades, purple events). Nodes have 3–5 levels, and a child opens when its parent reaches `need()` = min(3, parent max). Prices are set per market (shop ~40–600, chippy ~5k–25k, curry ~200k–1M, street ~20–60M, buffet ~1B+), so the bot ends a season with most of the tree. Fields: `p`, `x`/`y` (100×64 grid), `max`, `base`, `k`, `v(level)`, `f(value)`.
- Runs are capped at 25 seconds of real play (`MAXRUN`, owner request). Start time = 15s + Appetite/Bottomless/Pre-workout, capped at 25. Time gains (clocks, Second Helpings, Second Wind) go through `addTime()` and can only win back time lost to hits (`R.el` tracks elapsed time). If you haven't lost any time, a clock gives bonus kcal instead. Iron Will (top of the Time branch) is a chance to shrug off a hit. The boss always spawns by 45% of the run.
- Bosses can't be AFK'd: standing next to one for over 2.2s triggers a telegraphed "bump" shockwave (-2s), and below 50% HP they enrage (faster attacks and moves).
- Beating a section boss ends the run as a win (`R.win`): the character jumps with arms up (`drawCheer`) under "BOSS DOWN!" with confetti, and the feast is paid as bonus kcal. Characters (`CHARS`, `S.char`, picker in the Character modal) are free and purely cosmetic: options passed to `drawLifter` (sk, top:"bare", crop, pony, ears, muzzle, stem, visor, antenna, cap, whistle, bm). Boss attack kinds: spread, ring, spin, slam, charge, wave (gap), rain, homing, burst, boomer, bounce, mine. Each boss has its own mix. Goblins shoot per market (`GOB`/`goblinShot`).
- End of run: `R.ending` freezes play for 1.6s, zooms in, and the lifter kneels panting (`drawKneel`) under "OUT OF STEAM!". Then the results card slides up. Beating The Sunday Roast leads to the closing credits (`showCredits`, a CSS crawl) and the option to start the next season.
- Crits: every chomp has 5% (+Big Bite) to deal ×3. Belly Slam (`bslam`, base 4% per chomp) sends a shockwave ring that bites every food nearby and hits bosses (`bellySlam()`, drawn from `R.pounds`).
- Food goblins (`zone.mini`, flag `f.mini`) wander and shoot single shots on the way to the boss, and are edible for bonus kcal. Time drops (`CLOCK`, flag `f.clock`) add appetite when eaten.
- Each market's room is drawn in `buildRoom()` from `FLOORS[id]` and `WALLS[id]` (shop shelves and fridges, chippy tiles, menus and hot counter, curry arches and carpet, street night market, buffet table and chandeliers). Light fittings are drawn every frame in `drawLights()`. kcal scales with the market, so earlier markets pay less.
- Hazards are updated in `hazards()`: puddles/grease (slide or slow), fire spills, seagulls (steal food; run into them to shoo), scooters (warning lane, then a knock), waiters (bump them to drop food) and Big Dave (a rival eater). A knock means stun plus lost time, never death.
- The save is `localStorage` key `bulkSeason.v2` (`fresh()` lists the fields). Bump the key if the save shape changes incompatibly. "Save & devices" exports the whole save as a `BULK1:` + base64 code (copy or download) and imports one. This is how the owner moves between laptop and phone; there are no accounts.
- Save safety: every load copies the raw save to `bulkSeason.v2.backup` (Save & devices → Restore backup). If loading throws, `S.loadFailed` stops saving so the real save is never overwritten. A `storage` event from another tab sets `readOnly`, so an older tab can't clobber newer progress. When testing, block the old page's `setItem` before reloading, or its unload save overwrites the test save.
- Audio: `audio()` makes the context on the first click or key press. `SFX` respects `S.muted`, and the music respects `S.music` independently. Music is a tiny step sequencer (`SONGS.hub` / `SONGS.run`, 64 sixteenth-note steps of MIDI notes) scheduled by `musicTick`, with `setSong()` switching tracks per screen.
- A "How to play" modal opens on the first visit (`S.runs===0`).
- **Eat food, throw at enemies** (owner request, 2026-10-17). Walking into food eats it, but bosses and goblins are never eaten: the eat loop and dash skip `f.boss||f.mini`. Instead `throwing()` auto-fires dumbbells (`R.darts`, drawn by `drawDumbbell`) every `R.thrCd` (= chew × Fast Jaws / Quick Release, so chewing upgrades speed up throws too). `thrTarget()` picks the boss if it's within `thrR+260`, otherwise the nearest goblin within `thrR`, and fires nothing when no enemy is in range. The aimed dumbbell homes gently on its target; Twin Throw extras fan out without homing. A hit calls `chomp(f,{shot:1,amt,crit})`: damage = jaw × (boss: `R.dmg`) × `R.thrPow`, where thrPow = `THR` × `THRZ[market]` × Throw Power × Heavy Iron. `THR`/`THRZ` exist because throws never miss and never need you close; they were tuned with the sim so boss fights last about as long as chewing did (shop ~4s, chippy 6–10s, curry/street 8–15s, buffet 7–10s for the bot).
- Throwing nodes (yellow, grid-adjacent): tpow (1,1 under chew), quick (2,2 under combo) → trange (2,3) → tspd (2,4); quick → twin (3,2) → pierce (4,2) → heavy (5,2); trange → pdrop (3,3) → pump (3,4).
- Power-ups (`PUPS`, `R.pups`, `R.pw`, `powerups()`): rapid (×2.5 fire rate), spread (+4 dumbbells), ring (8 extra in a circle), heavy (×2.5 damage, +3 pierce, bigger). One spawns every `11-1.5×Power-up Drops` seconds and goblins drop one 12% (+6%/level) of the time. They last `Pumped` seconds (4 + level). Active ones show as timer rings above the player (`drawPwIcons`).
- Goblins now come in packs (3–7 depending on section and market), drift toward the player, bump you for 0.8s on contact, and each shoots a bit less often than before. Gym Buddies leave goblins alone.
- `startRun` / `update` / `chomp` / `eaten` / `draw` / `endRun` make up the run. A single `frame()` loop also polls the gamepad and animates the hub.
- Screens: a "Press any key/button" title (`#title`, `leaveTitle()`; a key or click also requests fullscreen), then the gym hub, the run, and modals. On landscape screens, `fitScreen()` adds `body.staged`: the hub is laid out at a fixed 1600×900 `#stage` and scaled with `--k` to fit (no scrolling, like a console game). The run HUD scales with `--hk`, and modal cards with `--mk`, so it all reads well on a TV. Landscape phones (`body.phone`, when the 1600×900 stage would scale below 0.62) get a smaller stage, `--sw`×`--sh`, that fills the screen at a readable scale. On phones nothing scrolls: `fitCard()` (a ResizeObserver on every `.mcard`) scales each menu card to fit the screen, and Help and Save get wide phone layouts. On phones the hub becomes one full-width panel. `phoneDom()` moves `#goBtn` into the top bar and moves the market card and `.notes` (tasks) into a phone-only Markets tab (`#paneMk`, `TAB==="markets"`). The tool buttons and footer collapse into a ☰ Menu modal (`#menuModal`), the stats card and gym canvas are hidden, and on touch, tapping a tree node first selects it (`touchSel`) and a second tap buys it. Portrait on a touch screen shows `#rotate` ("turn your phone sideways"), which is pure CSS via `orientation:portrait and pointer:coarse`, and rotating mid-run pauses. Portrait on a desktop browser still falls back to the scrolling layout. Scrollbars, text selection and the context menu are disabled. The cursor hides when a pad is in use or the mouse is idle during a run.
- The hub's tree and market list are built once. `renderGym()` only updates classes and text, so controller focus survives purchases.
- Prestige: once The Buffet is beaten, "New season" resets the season and grants medals (+15% kcal each, `medalsFor`). Boss HP scales ×1.6 per season.

## Controls
- Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, Space/Shift Dash, Q Inhale, E Plate Toss, R Cheat Meal, Esc/P pause, F fullscreen. Arrows and Enter navigate menus.
- Xbox pad: left stick or d-pad to move, A/RB Dash, B/LB Inhale, X Toss (aim with the right stick), Y Cheat, Start to pause. Rumble (`rumble()`) fires on knocks, golden food and bosses. In menus, d-pad/stick do spatial navigation (`navMove`), A presses, B backs out, LB/RB switch market, LT/RT (Q/E on keyboard) jump between the markets and the tree/gym lists (`jumpPanel`), and Start begins a run. In the tree the d-pad follows branches (`treeMove`), and holding A keeps buying the focused upgrade. A toast confirms when a controller connects. Browsers only expose a pad after one button press, and a pad press doesn't count as a user gesture for fullscreen, so use F or a click for that.
- Touch (`body.touch`, set on the first touch or on `pointer:coarse` devices): an on-screen controller. The first finger on the arena becomes a floating stick (`VS`, `#vstick`, follows the thumb past the rim), and the skills form an Xbox-style face-button cluster at bottom right (Dash = A, the biggest). Pause sits at bottom centre and reacts on pointerdown. The arena uses the full height under the HUD, and the controls overlay it. Tapping the title goes fullscreen (on `click`, since Chrome only grants fullscreen when a tap ends, not on touch pointerdown) and locks landscape where allowed (Android). A ⛶ Fullscreen button sits on the title (`#tfsBtn`) and beside pause during a run while not fullscreen (`#runFs`); `body.fs` / `body.nofs` (iPhone has no fullscreen API) control them. Touch controls scale with `--ck` on short screens. `manifest.webmanifest` (fullscreen, landscape) makes "Add to Home screen" launch like an app.
- On keyboard, Plate Toss aims at the mouse cursor if the mouse has moved recently. Otherwise it throws the way you're facing. Glyph hints switch with the last input device (`body.pad` / `body.touch`).

## Balance
Open `index.html#debug` and run `BS.sim(900)` in the console. A bot plays full seasons headlessly and buys the cheapest upgrade each time, then returns milestones (run number and minutes at each market open and boss kill). Tuning targets: the bot finishes season 1 in about 29–30 runs (about one new section per run, ~11 bot minutes since boss kills end runs early), so a person should take an hour or more. The sim log now shows `fight Ns` (how long each boss took): use it when touching throw damage. The sim also earns and spends gym money. `BS.sim(900,{season:2,medals:5})` checks later seasons: about 20 bot minutes each, earning 5–6 medals.

## Desktop (Electron)
- In the page, `window.desktop` (from preload) means the game is running on desktop. There, saves go through `desktop.load()`/`desktop.save()` to `%APPDATA%/Bulk Season/save.json` (an atomic tmp+rename write, with `save.backup.json` copied on every launch) instead of localStorage. A Quit button is shown (`#quitGame`, `#quitGame2`). A single-instance lock stops two copies from fighting over the save.
- F3 toggles an FPS counter.
- Benchmarks: `npm run fps` (uncapped, about 200 FPS) or `npx electron . --fps-vsync` (should hold 143–144 on a 144 Hz screen with about 2 dropped frames). They print `FPS TEST {...}` and use a temporary profile.
- Build: `npm run dist` → `dist/*-portable.exe` and `*-setup.exe`. They're unsigned. Set `CSC_IDENTITY_AUTO_DISCOVERY=false`.
- **In Claude's shell, `ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE=1` is set (VS Code)**, so always prefix electron/electron-builder commands with `env -u ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE`.
- Performance rule: the run HUD must only touch the DOM when a value changes. Use `hset(sel,prop,val)`, which is cached in `HC` and cleared in `startRun`. Bars use a `transform: scaleX` (`bar` prop), never `width`. Per-frame DOM writes are what cost the frame rate (117 → 144 FPS).
- Steam later: the plan is steamworks.js for achievements and Steam Cloud (point Cloud at save.json), a separate free Demo app, and real art in place of the emoji icon.
